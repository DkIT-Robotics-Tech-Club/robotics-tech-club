# Project Lattice - Hardware Research

## 1. Purpose

This document records research into the hardware options being considered for Project Lattice.

The purpose of the research is to identify hardware that is suitable for the project's requirements while considering performance, cost, availability, compatibility and ease of integration.

No hardware should be selected solely because it is the most powerful or most expensive option.

## 2. Research Areas

The hardware research will cover:

* Acoustic sensing
* Computer vision
* Embedded systems
* Processing
* Power
* Mechanical construction
* Supporting electronics

## 3. Acoustic Sensing

* Microphone types
* Number of microphones required
* Microphone-array layouts
* Analogue vs digital microphones
* Signal quality
* Sampling requirements
* Synchronisation
* Signal acquisition hardware
* ADC requirements
* Signal conditioning
* Expected operating environment

## 10. Evaluation Criteria

Hardware options should be compared using the following criteria:

* Technical performance
* Compatibility
* Cost
* Availability
* Documentation
* Ease of use
* Reliability
* Power requirements
* Development time
* Availability of replacement parts

The cheapest option should not automatically be selected, and the most expensive option should not automatically be considered the best.

## 11. Research Recommendations

Each research area should finish with a recommendation.

The recommendation should explain:

1. Which option is preferred.
2. Why it was selected.
3. What alternatives were considered.
4. What limitations exist.
5. Estimated cost.
6. What additional hardware may be required.
